## Overview

The `Badge` component renders a small, rounded label used to communicate status, category, or count. It supports two visual variants (`light` and `solid`), seven semantic colors, two sizes, and optional icon slots on either side.

## Import

```tsx
import Badge from "@/components/ui/badge/Badge";
```

## Props

<ParamField path="children" type="ReactNode" required>
  The text or content displayed inside the badge.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="variant" type="light | solid" default="light">
  - `light` — pastel background with colored text
  - `solid` — fully saturated background with white text
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="color" type="primary | success | error | warning | info | light | dark" default="primary">
  The color scale applied to the badge. Works with both `light` and `solid` variants.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="size" type="sm | md" default="md">
  - `sm` — `text-theme-xs`
  - `md` — `text-sm`
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="startIcon" type="ReactNode">
  An icon rendered to the left of the badge label.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="endIcon" type="ReactNode">
  An icon rendered to the right of the badge label.
</ParamField>

## Variants

### Light background

Passel backgrounds with colored text — suited for tables, status columns, and list items.

```tsx
<Badge variant="light" color="primary">Primary</Badge>
<Badge variant="light" color="success">Success</Badge>
<Badge variant="light" color="error">Error</Badge>
<Badge variant="light" color="warning">Warning</Badge>
<Badge variant="light" color="info">Info</Badge>
<Badge variant="light" color="light">Light</Badge>
<Badge variant="light" color="dark">Dark</Badge>
```

### Solid background

Fully saturated backgrounds with white text — suited for tags, chips, and prominent labels.

```tsx
<Badge variant="solid" color="primary">Primary</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid" color="success">Success</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid" color="error">Error</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid" color="warning">Warning</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid" color="info">Info</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid" color="light">Light</Badge>
<Badge variant="solid" color="dark">Dark</Badge>
```
